## 2. 会话中切换与可见效果

- 三个预设和自定义滑块都在既有会话中发送 OptionMessage。仅选择 custom 先保存档位，具体数值由滑块或关闭对话框提交。滑块有 1000 ms 防抖，预设菜单直接调用。对端更新画质比例、帧率上限；视频循环读取新的每帧间隔，支持动态改画质的编码器就地调用 `set_quality`。接口返回表示本地调用完成，没有“首张新画质帧已显示”的回执。[提交](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/common/widgets/dialog.dart#L1845-L1871) · [防抖](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/common/widgets/setting_widgets.dart#L24-L36) · [预设与自定义](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/client.rs#L3559-L3594) · [发送](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/ui_session_interface.rs#L485-L515) · [接收](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/connection.rs#L4856-L4884) · [编码循环](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_service.rs#L1426-L1440)
- 换编码偏好会重新上报解码能力。只有协商得到的实际编码改变时，对应检查才返回 `SWITCH`，视频服务可重建编码器；未重新拨号或要求重新登录。画质切换也有例外：编码器不支持热改（源码注明 VAAPI），且处于非 VBR、非自定义档时会触发视频切换。该分支对自定义档没有同样的强制重建。重建、接收关键帧、解码与绘制之间可能出现短暂停帧或闪动；没有固定生效时限或无黑屏保证。真机应分别比较单改预设、单改 FPS、实际 codec 改变，记录操作至连续新帧的时间与画面中断。[能力更新](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/ui_session_interface.rs#L562-L565) · [服务端协商](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/connection.rs#L4876-L4884) · [编码选择](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_service.rs#L651-L676) · [切换条件](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_service.rs#L737-L760) · [热改例外](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_service.rs#L1428-L1440) · [服务重入](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/service.rs#L301-L325)

## 4. 核心已有的自动调节

被控端 `VideoQoS` 以 TestDelay 回包、相对 RTT 基线的排队延迟及长期未回包调 FPS，跨观察者取较低帧率。ABR 在 `enable-abr != N` 且各显示编码器都支持热改时启用，约每 3 秒按拥塞和画面活动调节码率比例。最近一次用户设置定义目标，并可立即重置当前比例。控制端另按解码队列和解码速度发送 `custom_fps` 上限。静止画面低 FPS 也可能是正常现象。[算法与时钟](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L9-L66) · [ABR 条件](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L387-L403) · [画质写入](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L435-L454) · [多用户目标](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L677-L685) · [最低帧率](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L818-L835) · [控制端限帧](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/client/io_loop.rs#L1420-L1469)

**工程判断。** 按每个延迟/FPS 更新反复切档会把刚降下的比例重新拉回档位目标，还可能影响同屏的其他客服。优先考虑“自动”选择一次基础档位后交给已有自适应。若产品确需界面自动换档，使用多次新鲜样本、不同的升降阈值、冷却时间和手动优先；避免以单次低 FPS 触发换档，避免自动更换 codec。阈值和实际收益要经动态与静止内容、直连和中继的对照测量。[目标重置](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L449-L454) · [跨用户降码率](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/server/video_qos.rs#L731-L756) · [控制端队列处理](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/client/io_loop.rs#L1420-L1449)

## 5. 断线后的重连、窗口和输入

- 普通错误由核心生成可重试条件，Flutter 仅对可重试错误安排倒计时。起始 1 秒，随后 2、4、8……，这里没有三次上限；成功拿到包含显示器的 peer info 后复位。已有会话的特定 offline 错误约 30 秒内可再试，末次倒计时可越过窗口；首次连接离线不满足此例外。错误密码进入输入流程，预览明确排除了不安全连接重试。[错误过滤](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/src/client.rs#L5169-L5186) · [消息分类](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/models/model.dart#L905-L917) · [离线例外](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/models/model.dart#L996-L1024) · [初值](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/models/model.dart#L119-L125) · [计时器](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/models/model.dart#L1081-L1096) · [复位](https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/blob/fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17/flutter/lib/models/model.dart#L1447-L1453) · [预览安全门槛](https://github.com/Kingjames020323/steaddesk-client/blob/09f2424ae6187573878109ec69bd504048c3e3e2/patches/0004-refuse-insecure.patch#L160-L172)
